## 4 Agent Packages (Maps to Nick Peru's Agency Model)

### 1. Content Agent ($297/mo)
- Skills: content-factory, social-media, niche-analyzer, brand-scraper
- Channels: telegram (approval queue), email (calendar)
- Schedule: Mon research, Tue generate, Thu publish
- Replaces: $2-5K/mo agency retainer

### 2. Lead Gen Agent ($297/mo)
- Skills: prospect-research, cold-email, email-sender, calendar, crm
- Channels: email (outbound), voice (warm call), whatsapp (follow-up), sms (reminders)
- Triggers: email open → call, link click → case study, no reply → SMS
- Replaces: $3-5K/mo agency retainer

### 3. Funnel Agent ($297-497/mo)
- Skills: funnel-automation, landing-pages, email-sequences, sms-sequences, calendar, ad-copy-generator, analytics
- Channels: web (landing pages), email (nurture), sms, voice (qualification)
- Replaces: GoHighLevel + $3-5K/mo agency

### 4. Voice Qualifier Agent ($97-497/mo)
- Skills: voice-agent-builder, call-analytics, telephony, calendar, crm
- Channels: voice (inbound + outbound), sms (follow-up), whatsapp
- Playbook: opt-in → call 60s → qualify → book or nurture
- Replaces: $500-1.5K/mo add-on

## Agency Channel Model

```
Nick's Student (Agency Owner)
└── Signs up for OpenClaw Agency ($997/mo)
    ├── Gets white-labeled platform
    ├── Deploys agents for their clients
    ├── Charges clients $2-5K/mo
    └── Every call/SMS flows through EPIC's pipes
```

100 agencies × 10 clients = 1,000 businesses
1,000 × ~2,000 min/mo = 2M minutes/mo
2M × $0.12 profit = **$240K/mo telecom alone**
